Skyport Tycoon privacy policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

In short: Skyport Tycoon has no accounts and doesn’t ask for any personal details. Your game progress is saved on your own device. Optional rewarded ads come from Google AdMob, and optional purchases are handled by Google Play.

This policy explains what information the Skyport Tycoon mobile game (“the game”) handles and why. The game is made by Underline Studio (“we”, “us”). It applies to the Android version of the game distributed through Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in your device’s local storage. This includes your coins, gems, airports, upgrades, crew, research, tasks, daily streak, sound and display settings, and a record of purchases so they can be applied in the game.

We do not send this data to our own servers, and we do not operate any servers that receive it.

Information we don’t collect

We don’t ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. The game has no sign-in or account system.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads, which play only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect ads against fraud, AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • your device model, operating system and app version
  • how you interact with ads

Google handles this information under its own policies, described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases, such as gem packs and the Airline Pass, are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. Google tells the game which item you bought so it can be delivered and restored, and Google’s privacy policy applies to the payment itself.

Community and support

If you contact us through Discord or our website, we receive whatever you choose to share, such as your username and messages, and use it only to respond to you. Discord’s own privacy policy applies to your use of Discord.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time in the game under Settings, then Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data in Android settings. Information held by Google for advertising or purchases is managed through your Google account and Android settings.

Security

Game data never leaves your device through our own systems. Communication with Google’s advertising and payment services is encrypted in transit.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we don’t hold personal information about players ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google for advertising and purchase data. We are happy to help with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
